# SBA-Analyzer

## Goal
Find steam accounts that are likely to have been sold, or to be sold in the future

- Bot accounts signs
- - Account age vs play time is unrealistic for a normal person
- - Friends are added too quickly
- - Faceit rank vs playtime is unrealistic
- - Very old accounts 10+ years with few hours played
- - Accounts with very few games 1 - 4 depending on account age 
- - Accounts that are friends with an unusual amount of accounts that meet the criteria above
- - Accounts that has unusally high precentage of banned friends
